Pixel-art sprite sheet request.

Below are 9 reference sprite sheets from one game, written as text. Each sheet is
144x144 pixels: a grid of 6 columns x 6 rows of 24x24 frames. Every character in a
line is one pixel. "." is transparent. Digits 1-9 point to that sheet's palette.
No semi-transparent pixels, no anti-aliasing.

Rows of every sheet (same order, same frame counts, frames start at the left):
  row 1  idle   4 frames
  row 2  walk   6 frames
  row 3  hurt   4 frames (the 3rd frame is a solid white flash silhouette)
  row 4  jump   3 frames
  row 5  bow    1 frame
  row 6  dash   6 frames
Unused cells stay transparent.

Style shared by all references: the character faces right, a chunky 2-3 heads tall
body, a dark 1px outline (palette 1), a body color plus a darker shade, a small
bright eye that contrasts with the head, 4-5 colors total, feet on the same baseline.

Make ONE new sheet in exactly this format for a NEW animal: a CAT.
Same 144x144 size, same 6x6 grid, same rows and frame counts, same proportions,
pixel density, outline and motion as the references (reuse their body motion
frame by frame). Show pointed ears and a tail so it reads as a cat at 24x24.
Output a PNG with a transparent background, no scaling, no text, no grid lines.
